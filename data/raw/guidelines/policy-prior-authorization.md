# Prior Authorization Policy for High-Cost Medicines (Illustrative Operational Policy)

> Synthetic document written for this demo platform. It describes a fictional organisation's process.

## Scope
Prior authorization is required before dispensing GLP-1 receptor agonists, SGLT2 inhibitors when prescribed for weight management alone, biologic therapies, and any medicine costing more than EUR 500 per month. Metformin, ACE inhibitors, statins and standard inhalers do not require prior authorization.

## Required documentation
A GLP-1 prior authorization request must include the most recent HbA1c result (within the last 3 months), current BMI, a list of diabetes medicines already tried with dates, and the prescriber's clinical justification. Requests for SGLT2 inhibitors must include the latest eGFR.

## Turnaround times
A standard prior authorization request is decided within 5 business days of receiving complete documentation. An urgent request, where a delay could seriously jeopardise the patient's health, is decided within 24 hours.

## Incomplete requests
If a prior authorization request is missing documentation, it is pended and the prescriber is notified within 1 business day. A pended request is closed as withdrawn if the missing information is not received within 14 calendar days.

## Appeals
A denied request can be appealed by the prescriber within 30 days. Appeals are reviewed by a clinical pharmacist who was not involved in the original decision.
