# Patient Data Handling and AI Use Policy (Illustrative Governance Policy)

> Synthetic document written for this demo platform. It describes a fictional organisation's policy, loosely modelled on GDPR and HIPAA principles.

## Minimum necessary access
Staff and systems must access only the minimum necessary patient information required for the task. Role-based access controls apply to all clinical systems, including AI assistants.

## Use of external AI services
Directly identifying patient information (name, address, phone number, date of birth, medical record number) must not be sent to an external AI service unless the service is covered by a data processing agreement and approved by the Data Protection Officer. Otherwise, data must be de-identified before it leaves the organisation's environment.

## Audit logging
All access to patient records, including access by AI systems, must be logged with user, time, purpose, and records accessed. Audit logs of record access are retained for 7 years.

## Suspected breaches
If a data breach is suspected, the clinician must report it to the Data Protection Officer immediately. The organisation must notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach that is likely to put individuals' rights at risk.

## AI output review
AI-generated content that will enter the patient record or be sent to a patient must be reviewed by a qualified clinician. AI assistants must cite the sources they used and must not provide individual diagnoses or dosing instructions directly to patients.
