# Standard Operating Procedure: Discharge Summaries (Illustrative SOP)

> Synthetic document written for this demo platform. It describes a fictional organisation's process.

## Timeliness
The discharge summary must be sent electronically to the patient's GP within 48 hours of discharge. For discharges on a Friday or before a public holiday, the summary must be sent before the patient leaves the ward.

## Required sections
Every discharge summary must include: reason for admission, diagnoses (coded in ICD-10), procedures performed, medication on discharge, medication changes, allergies, follow-up arrangements, and pending test results.

## Medication changes
Every medication that was started, stopped, or changed in dose during the admission must be listed with the reason for the change. Unexplained medication changes are the most common cause of GP queries and readmission-related errors.

## Sign-off
The discharge summary is drafted by the discharging doctor and must be signed off by the responsible consultant or their delegated registrar. AI-drafted summaries are permitted only as a first draft and must be reviewed and signed off by the consultant before sending.

## Patient copy
The patient receives a plain-language copy at discharge, including warning signs that should prompt them to seek urgent care.
